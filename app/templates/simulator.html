          What a model run can tell you about Kuala Lumpur and Singapore

          Model runs use observed daily fire emissions from Sumatra and Kalimantan where they are bundled (1997–2023 and this week), and Kalimantan’s synthesised season clusters otherwise. Studies disagree about how much of Singapore’s haze comes from each island: climatological studies favour Sumatra, while one using 2015’s actual winds found Central Kalimantan as important for west Singapore and dominant in the east. The model runs high near burning peat, misses some days, and uses a monthly wind average for this year, so treat its KL and Singapore numbers as indicative. For what actually happened, use This week.

                The weather

                Whether this smoke reaches you depends on the wind. A wind from the south carries Kalimantan's smoke into Sarawak; it takes a wind from the east-southeast to carry it to Kuala Lumpur and Singapore. The model uses one wind for the whole map, so pick it here — the arrow on the map shows it.
